# Evaluation data provenance

`insurance/` contains public insurance brochures, not complete policy contracts.
Evaluation labels were manually curated from text extracted with pypdf 5.6.0;
`page` always means the 1-based **physical PDF page**, not a printed folio. Exact
anchors are stored in `evaluation/*.json`. They do not invent clauses, policy
sections, effective dates, or unstated terms.

The files were obtained from the pinned public reference corpus below. These
URLs provide reproducible byte provenance; the documents identify HDFC ERGO or
the other issuing insurer on their face. Product availability and wording may
have changed since the corpus was collected.

Base URL: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abhinav221294/agentic-ai-projects/b4274ba677ec793dd98251e3f7c8a0c34af009a4/finshield_ai/data/insurance/`

`synthetic_bills/` contains 12 wholly fictional, deterministic PDFs generated by
`scripts/generate_bills.py`. Their gold fields are used only by retrieval-only
mode; full mode gives the PDF itself to the claim agent. No real person or claim
appears in these fixtures.

## Text-only limitation

The supplied `LIC_Jeevan-Labh_Brochure_9-inch-x-8-inch_Eng.pdf` has no extractable
text under PyPDF. It is retained unchanged for corpus provenance but intentionally
rejected by the application (OCR is out of scope). The core evaluation therefore
uses 15 cases across the other ten text-readable brochures. Rejecting the scanned
brochure is an input-validation outcome, not a fabricated RAGAS score.
